Teaching

Making economics
accessible and relevant.

I have supported undergraduate and postgraduate teaching at the University of Auckland Business School, with an emphasis on active learning, clear communication, and using real-world problems to strengthen economic reasoning.

Teaching approach

Learning through questions, collaboration and application

I aim to build an inclusive environment in which students feel comfortable asking questions and testing ideas. I use interactive activities, applied case studies, and constructive feedback to help students develop confidence and connect economic frameworks to contemporary events.

01

Active learning

Interactive simulations, group discussion, and problem-based activities encourage students to apply ideas rather than memorise results.

02

Real-world relevance

Contemporary examples from trade, technology, and public policy connect economics to decisions students encounter.

03

Inclusive support

Clear expectations, approachable consultations, and timely feedback support learners from different backgrounds.
