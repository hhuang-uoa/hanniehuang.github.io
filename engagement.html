School engagement · 2025–2026
Competition administration · Outreach

New Zealand Economics Competition

As a competition administrator, I have supported the organisation of the New Zealand Economics Competition, including school registration and communications, online competition administration, awards ceremony logistics, and coordination of undergraduate volunteers.

In 2026, more than 2,500 students from 69 schools participated. The awards evening brought together students, teachers, families, and university representatives to celebrate achievement and encourage interest in economics.
