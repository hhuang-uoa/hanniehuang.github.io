01 / Introduction

About me

I am a PhD candidate in the Department of Economics, University of Auckland Business School. I am also affiliated with the New Zealand APEC Study Centre (NZASC). I am on the 2026–27 academic job market.

My current work focuses on privacy, institutional trust, innovation, and economic growth in the data economy. Beyond research, I contribute to undergraduate and postgraduate teaching, collaborative research projects, and academic engagement activities.
